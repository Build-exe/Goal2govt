About Us

Hi! We are Team Build.exe

Goal2Govt is a student-built platform created by Team Build.exe, dedicated to helping students and job seekers achieve their government career goals. We provide curated mock tests, previous year papers, career roadmaps, and exam updates to support every aspirant on their journey to success.

  1. 1

    Our Mission

    To make government exam preparation simple, structured and accessible — guiding every aspirant with clear roadmaps and reliable resources at every stage of their journey.
